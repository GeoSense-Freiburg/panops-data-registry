Summary

The full EFP analysis (XGBoost and LightGBM, Optuna-tuned, repeated leave-one-site-out, TreeSHAP) was rerun on the deadtrees v5 maps with exactly the same build rules and code as the v4.1 paper dataset. This report shows every v5 result next to v4.1. Nothing in the paper or the coauthor draft was changed.
