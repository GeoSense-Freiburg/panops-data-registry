What the metrics mean

Relative mortality: share of the tree canopy that is dead or was lost since last year, (deadwood + tree cover loss) / (tree cover + tree cover loss) × 100.

Relative deadwood: standing deadwood relative to the living canopy, deadwood / tree cover × 100; empty when tree cover < 10 %.

Absolute deadwood: standing deadwood cover as a share of the buffer area.

Deadwood gain: new standing deadwood since the previous year, in percentage points.

Relative tree cover loss: canopy lost since the previous year relative to last year's tree cover; empty when that tree cover < 10 %.

The three change metrics compare a year with the previous one, so they start in 2018. At sites with little tree cover, deadwood values of a few percent are mostly map noise. Full definitions and caveats are in README.md.
